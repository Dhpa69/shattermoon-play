HOW TO PLAY

Computer

  • WASD move · Shift sprint
  • Mouse aim · Left click fire · Right click aim down sights
  • Space jump · press again in air to double-jump · hold to glide
  • R reload · 1 2 3 / wheel swap weapon
  • Q grenade · C melee · F Super
  • Tab gear · Esc pause

Controller (PS5 / Xbox / Backbone)

  • L stick move (click to sprint) · R stick aim
  • R2/RT fire · L2/LT aim down sights
  • ✕/A jump · □/X reload · hold to revive · △/Y swap
  • L1/LB grenade · R1/RB or ○/B melee · L1+R1 Super
  • D-pad ↑ heavy · ← → weapons · ↓ emote · Options pause · Share/View gear

iPhone

  • Left thumb anywhere on the left: move (push far to sprint)
  • Right thumb drag: aim (you can drag on FIRE too)
  • FIRE hold to shoot · ⤒ jump / double / hold to glide
  • ✸ grenade · ✊ melee · ✦ Super when full
  • ⇄ swap · ⟳ reload · ◎ aim down sights

Survive the waves, aim for the glowing heads (yellow numbers = critical hits), and break shields faster with a matching element. Kills charge your Super. Grab the spinning engrams enemies drop — they become new weapons. Open Gear to equip them and raise your Power. Wave 6 brings the boss.
